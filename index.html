Privacy Policy

What the game stores, what leaves your device, and who else is involved. This describes how the app actually behaves, not what it might do later.

What kind of app this is

Realmfront: Conquest is a free, single-player strategy game. It has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions: nothing in the game can be bought with money. Everything that another game would sell — extra points for permanent upgrades, and the bonuses in the shop — is earned by playing, or by choosing to watch an advertisement. The game is supported entirely by advertising, described below.

No account, no server

The game has no login, no registration and no player profile anywhere. There is no Realmfront account, which is also why there is no account-deletion page: see Deleting your data for what deletion means here. Matches are played against the game's own opponents on your device; no game server is involved and none of your progress is uploaded to us.

What is stored on your device

The following is kept in the app's own local storage, on your phone. We do not run a game server, so this copy is not sent to us:

  • Your chosen language and your banner colour.
  • How far you have got in the campaign — the highest chapter unlocked and which loop of the campaign you are on.
  • Your points and permanent upgrades — the meta currency you have earned and the level of each upgrade you have bought with it.
  • Which one-off hints you have already seen, so the game stops repeating them.
  • Your music setting.

None of this contains a name, an email address, a contact, a photo, a file, a message or a location. It is not tied to any identifier that would let anyone recognise you.

Advertising

Realmfront shows advertisements through Unity LevelPlay, Unity's mediation SDK, together with the ad networks whose adapters are included in the build. Ads appear in two forms:

  • Rewarded video you choose to watch. Offered in exchange for extra points, or for the bonuses the shop grants. It never starts by itself: it plays only if you tap the button offering it, and the reward is given only if the advertisement is actually watched through. Declining costs nothing.
  • An occasional full-screen advertisement between matches.

To select, deliver, measure and secure those advertisements, the ad services can process: your advertising identifier, your IP address and a rough location derived from it (typically the country), device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version, network type), and information about the advertisement itself — that it was requested, shown, clicked or closed. This processing is done by those companies under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource) — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Unity's dashboard can change which advertisements and placements are served without an app update. Adding a genuinely new advertising network is different: it means putting its adapter into the build, which means a new version of the app, an update to this page, and an update to the Data safety section in Google Play before it goes out.

Your choice about personalised advertising

Where the law requires it — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and the US states with their own rules — the game asks for your choice before advertising starts, using the consent screen provided by the advertising SDK. You can decide not to allow personalised advertising; you will still see advertisements, but they will not be chosen using your advertising identifier in the same way.

Independently of the game, Android lets you reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer and Android version).

Children

Realmfront: Conquest is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that involved their personal data, write to the address above and we will act on it.

Deleting your data

Everything the game itself keeps is on your device, so uninstalling the app removes it. Clearing the app's data from the Android settings has the same effect and leaves the app installed. Because there is no account and no server copy, there is nothing further for us to delete on our side.

Data already processed by the advertising services is held by them, not by us. Their privacy policy, linked above, describes how to ask them about it.

Where the map comes from

The campaign maps are generated from public-domain geographic data and drawn on your device. The game does not read your location, and the region you play has nothing to do with where you are.

Permissions

The game needs internet access, and that is used for advertising only. It does not ask for the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this page does not describe — a new ad network, analytics, anything of that kind — this page is updated before that version reaches players, and the date at the top changes with it.
